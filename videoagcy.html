Eventy

Eventy is n event planner website is a professional platform designed to showcase, organize, and manage events like weddings, parties, or corporate functions. It serves three main purposes:

  • Portfolio: Displays past events and client reviews to build trust.
  • Services: Details event packages, from full-service coordination to decor design.
  • Booking: Features contact forms for clients to request quotes and book consultations.

EventyPro

Corporate Events: Business-focused gatherings designed to motivate teams, launch products, or network (e.g., conferences, seminars, galas, team buildings).

HappyEventy

Social Events: Personal and family celebrations centered around emotion and milestones (e.g., weddings, birthdays, anniversaries, baby showers).

EventUp

Public Events: Large-scale, community-focused experiences open to the general public (e.g., festivals, concerts, charity fundraisers, exhibitions).
